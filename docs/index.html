Common credit situations

You are not the only one

Most people we talk to have at least one of these on their report. Here is what each one means, in plain words.

Collections

An unpaid bill that was sent to a collection agency. How it affects you depends on the type, the amount, and how recent it is. Some medical collections are treated differently than others. A loan officer can look at yours and explain your options.

Late payments

A payment reported 30 or more days past due. Recent late payments weigh more than older ones. A steady record of on-time payments from here forward is one of the most helpful things you can build.

High utilization

Using a large share of your available credit card limits. High balances compared to your limits can pull your score down. Paying balances lower is often one of the faster ways to see movement.

Thin file

Very little credit history, or none at all. This is common for younger buyers and people new to the country. Some loan programs can consider other payment history, such as rent and utilities.
